    Fair play

    Who gets to shuffle? Everyone.

    ♠

    A shared shuffle

    Each player mixes the encrypted deck in turn. A proof lets Midnight check that all 52 cards survived each shuffle, while the new order stays secret.

    ♦

    Your cards to play

    Your device opens your two hole cards. The table unlocks the flop, turn and river as betting progresses. You can even choose to show a bluff.

    ♣

    A pot that adds up

    The contract checks legal bets and who can win each pot. When hands tie, it checks the split down to the last odd chip.

    For players

    Your first game

    Getting to the river

    Choose + your name, save a profile, then select Practice table. The same poker contract runs inside your browser against five bots. Practice chips stay in that game.

    You get two private cards. The flop adds three shared cards, then the turn and river add one each. Make the strongest five-card hand from those seven, or win earlier if everyone else folds.

    Check to stay in for free when you owe nothing. Call to match the bet, raise to increase it, or fold to give up your claim on this pot.

    Bring friends to a Live table

    Each move goes to Midnight. The contract checks that it follows the rules before play advances.

    1. Each player needs a separate dust.poker profile and a Ledger 9 compatible Lace wallet on the same test network. Pick Preview or Preprod. Set Lace to use a prover on your device.
    2. Request test NIGHT at your unshielded address and select Generate tDUST in Lace. Allow time for tDUST to accrue for transaction fees. A seat takes 200 STAR for your stack and 200 STAR as a bond. One test NIGHT is one million STAR. Midnight funding guide
    3. The host connects Lace and opens a new table under Play with friends. Choose Copy invite link in the lobby or at the table. Guests can see the seats before choosing Join table. Play begins with two seated players.
    4. Keep the tab open for wallet prompts. To withdraw, choose Cash out and wait for Seat closed. Returning to the menu leaves your chips at the table. Keep your browser profile until you have withdrawn.

    Choose a table · Network and Lace settings

    Play with people drawn from the public queue

    Connect Lace and choose Find a public seat. Entries close every five minutes. With four or more entrants, the draw makes at least two tables of two to six players. With fewer, you can try a later draw. Entering costs a network fee. Your buy-in stays in your wallet until you accept your seat.

    One player at each table is drawn to open it. Come back with the same browser profile to take your seat or withdraw your chips. The first deal waits for everyone assigned to arrive. You can cash out while waiting. If someone withdraws before everyone arrives, that table closes to further buy-ins and each seated player can cash out.

    For protocol readers

    Inside a verified deal

    What does a shuffle prove?

    Live players publish fresh Jubjub public keys. Their sum is the ElGamal key for the deck. In seat order, each player privately permutes all 52 ciphertexts and re-encrypts them. The Compact shuffle circuit checks the permutation with a Poseidon-derived grand product and checks the re-encryption of every card.

    A valid permutation may be predictable. Deck randomness relies on at least one player choosing a fresh, uniform secret shuffle. Players can still share information outside the game. Use a prover you control because it receives the private inputs.

    Who picks my opponents?

    In a public draw, the Midnight queue fixes the entrants before a scheduled drand quicknet round. Your browser verifies the beacon signature and computes the same groups as the other players. It checks the assigned roster against the table’s locked opening before buying in. Friends can still exchange information or enter with multiple profiles. A player who stays away can delay their table. How drand works

    Who can read what?

    For a hole card, the other seats publish decryption shares. The holder supplies the last share locally. For board cards the required shares become public street by street. Stacks, bets and transaction timing are public.

    At a normal showdown, show_hand verifies the score of five distinct cards chosen from seven. The client picks its best five. The public score and board can narrow down which cards were held. If betting ends all-in before the board runs out, the remaining hands are tabled.

    How does the pot settle?

    settle checks eligibility in each side pot, ties, odd-chip order and conservation of the pot. Missed betting deadlines can trigger a fold. Missing a required protocol step can cost a seat its bond. The indexer reports state and transactions. Midnight verifies the contract calls.

    Reproduce the checks

    From a checkout with Bun installed, run the contract tests, generate the keys, then prove a complete set of calls. CI proves the 19 invite-table operations, public registration, table announcements and reserved-seat claims.

    bun install --frozen-lockfile
bun run check
bun run compact:build --zk
bun run prove:deal
